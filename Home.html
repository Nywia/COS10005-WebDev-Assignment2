About me

Interests

I tend to enjoy playing games (digital and physical), reading books, listening to white noise, cooking, coding and watching anime.

Background

From NZ, I have a bachelors of Software Engineering and some industry experience already and I'm not sure why I'm taking another bachelors for Cybersecurity given that I kind of already know most of the beginner level stuff. The networking stuff with switches and routers is the only part I actually enjoy due to not having prior knowledge and being adjacent to what I've learnt.

I mainly just want a job to do with software engineering but the job market isn't so good right now and I decided that instead of sitting around I can study while I try and find a job in the meantime.

Personality

Depending on who you ask and how long I've known someone for, people would typically say I'm a rather chill and nonchalant person. I used to show little to no emotion and had to learn how to express emotion via my facial expressions since seldom do I express it outwardly, keeping most thoughts to myself.

I'd say I'm a introvert but recently as I've gotten older I've started to try and be more extraverted and social since otherwise I would be a 24/7 homebody.

Student Life

I quite literally only show up to labs and have yet to attend a single lecture. Doing well enough grade wise. The main thing I struggle with would just be motivation to actually do the work even if I know what and how to do it.

Intro To Programming for example was far too easy that I ended up spending more time helping those around me and teaching them than actually doing any work. I also just did whatever was most "efficient". If you did every task for the portfolio, you'd get a Distinction (70 points) and it took me like 2-10 mins to do most tasks. So that's what I did, minimal time for maximum grades. Going beyond that required interviews and exponential time investment that just wasn't worth the effort.

Another thing is I want to be more social and make more friends, university is a perfect place to meet new people and I want to try and make the most of it (It's hard given my personality)
